# Ava × Master Yourself First — CreatorHub Launch Test

## Goal

Use Ava as CreatorHub's first end-to-end monetization test creator for a self-mastery digital product.

Minimum live money loop:

Ava content -> CreatorHub product page -> Stripe checkout -> secure ebook delivery -> workbook/challenge upsell -> Fanvue subscription/accountability layer.

## Test Creator

**Name:** Ava  
**Type:** Fictional adult AI creator  
**Visual direction:** Attractive blonde, polished Americana/lifestyle aesthetic; confident and approachable rather than political-expert styling.  
**Niche:** Self-mastery / masculine personal development / lifestyle  
**Tone:** Confident, playful, direct, challenging, warm, occasionally teasing; never preachy.  

### Audience

Adults who value strength, independence, family, discipline, personal responsibility, loyalty, earned respect, and practical self-improvement.

### Content pillars

- Self-mastery
- Discipline
- Earned respect
- Family and trust
- Independent thinking
- Leadership by example
- Confidence without ego

### Guardrails

- Do not present Ava as a licensed therapist or political authority.
- Do not use covert political persuasion.
- Do not shame or diagnose the audience.
- Keep personal-development claims practical and non-medical.
- Clearly present Ava as a fictional AI creator where platform rules require disclosure.

## Affiliate-ready model

The first product is marked affiliate-ready in product metadata with a proposed default commission of **40%**.

Future CreatorHub marketplace behavior:

- Product owner enables affiliate promotion.
- Creator clicks **Promote**.
- CreatorHub generates a unique tracked link/code.
- CreatorHub attributes clicks, sales, refunds, and commission.
- Product owner retains IP and controls commission terms.
- CreatorHub can later take a platform fee when third-party product owners are supported.

The first Ava launch is a validation case before opening the marketplace to outside creators.

## Ava launch content angles

Use short-form hooks that challenge behavior without diagnosing the viewer.

Examples:

- "You want respect? Cool. Here's the part nobody likes talking about."
- "A man who can't control his temper isn't intimidating. He's predictable."
- "If somebody you've never met online can ruin your whole afternoon, who is actually in control?"
- "Strength isn't winning every argument. Sometimes it's not needing the last word."
- "If you believe in personal responsibility, your excuses don't get a special exemption."
- "Protecting your family means more than protecting the house from what is outside it."

Every hook should lead to a practical lesson and then a clear CTA to `Master Yourself First` or the free challenge.
